Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ColoringPixelsOnline. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect your personal data while you use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

When you register for an account, sign in via Google, or save your progress, we collect basic details such as your email address, display name, account authentication tokens, and your pixel art canvas saves stored in our database.

3. How We Use Your Data

We do not share, rent, or sell your data to any third parties, advertisers, or outside corporations under any circumstances. We strictly use your information for the following limited purposes:

  • Customization Purposes: To personalize your experience, remember your username, display your custom profile details, and load your personal cloud-saved artwork.
  • Communication & Verification: To send account verification emails, password reset links, or essential service-related notifications necessary for operating your account securely.

4. Data Security

We rely on secure backend infrastructure (Firebase and Google Cloud security standards) to protect your credentials and prevent unauthorized data access.
